Why Won't AI Mention My Product

Many AI crawlers do not run JavaScript. Why Won't AI requests up to 10 of your pages as GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot and CCBot, one at a time, and shows what each crawler received.

The score has six parts: crawl and render, indexable body copy, intent match, internal linking, demand capture and AI citability. You see the score and the top 3 issues without an account. A free account gives the full report.

Remote MCP server

Run the checks from Claude Code, Cursor or VS Code. Server: https://mcp.whywontai.com/ (Streamable HTTP). check_robots needs no key. The scan tools need an API key from a free account.

claude mcp add --transport http whywontai https://mcp.whywontai.com/ \
  --header "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY"
