How to Use a Robots.txt Checker to Test Allow and Block Rules

A robots.txt checker allows you to test whether search engine bots and AI crawlers are permitted to access your website. By validating your rules, you ensure that search engines properly index your content while keeping unwanted traffic away.

Understanding the Robots.txt File

The robots.txt file sits in the root directory of your website and communicates with web crawlers. It uses 'Allow' and 'Disallow' directives to tell specific user-agents which pages they can or cannot visit. A misconfigured file can accidentally block legitimate search engines from seeing your pages.

How a Robots.txt Checker Works

A robots.txt testing tool simulates a specific crawler visiting a designated URL on your site. The checker reads your exclusion rules and tells you immediately whether the crawler has access or is blocked. This helps you catch syntax errors before search engines encounter them.

Testing Traditional Search vs. AI Crawlers

Traditional testers focus on major search engines like Googlebot and Bingbot. However, modern websites must also account for AI crawlers like GPTBot, ClaudeBot, and PerplexityBot. Ensuring these bots are correctly handled prevents visibility issues in generative search engines.
